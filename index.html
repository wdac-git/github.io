Cheap Eats Lab Growth Engine

A private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Cheap Eats Lab (Filipino and budget home cooking, filmed in Alberta, Canada).

It runs inside the owner's own Google Sheet and helps manage the channel's own videos: it reads the channel's statistics, writes titles and descriptions for new uploads, schedules them, and keeps a record of changes. It is not offered to anyone else.
