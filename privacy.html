Privacy Policy

Cheap Eats Lab Growth Engine · last updated October 3, 2026

Who uses this app

Cheap Eats Lab Growth Engine is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Cheap Eats Lab to manage that channel. It is not offered to the public, and no other person's account is connected to it.

What data it accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), it reads and updates the Cheap Eats Lab channel's own YouTube data: video titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, publishing schedule, playlists and channel statistics (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API). It reads files the owner places in the owner's own Google Drive folders for this purpose.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to manage the Cheap Eats Lab channel. It is stored in the owner's own Google Sheet and Google Drive. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising. Text and images of the channel's own new videos may be sent to Google's Gemini API to draft titles and descriptions.

Keeping or deleting data

The owner can disconnect the app at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections and delete the sheet and folders. Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
